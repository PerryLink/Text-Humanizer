I love writing code. It's fun and rewarding.

When I write, I keep things simple. Short sentences work best. They're easy to read. People understand them quickly.

AI tools are great for coding. But they make writing sound weird. Too formal. Too complex. Nobody talks like that.

This tool helps fix that problem. It checks your text. It tells you if you sound human. Or if you sound like a robot.

Try it yourself. Write something. Run the check. See your score. Make it better.
